# Planning 3D-printed cosplay armor that fits

A digital model may look right on screen yet need adjustments for movement, clothing and assembly. Use this checklist before committing to a large print.

## Fit checklist

1. **Take consistent measurements.** Record the body dimensions relevant to each part while wearing the clothing that will be underneath. Recheck each measurement and note units.
2. **Allow for movement and assembly.** Consider bending, sitting, ventilation, attachment straps and how pieces overlap. A fitted static model alone cannot validate movement or comfort.
3. **Check scale when importing and exporting.** Confirm a known dimension in your 3D software and again in the slicer. Do not assume a file's units match the software's scene units.
4. **Make a small physical test.** Print a narrow sizing ring, section or low-material mock-up before producing a full piece. Adjust the model after trying it on safely.
5. **Plan the print and joining.** Check build volume, orientation, support requirements and seams. Design connector and fastening tests for your printer and material.
