01 / Model preparation

Split a model for 3D printing

1. Check the model

Keep a copy of the original. Inspect for holes, internal faces, intersections and non-manifold geometry before cutting.

2. Plan cuts and joints

Measure usable build volume, allow room for supports and place connectors where there is enough material and access for assembly.

3. Test fit

Confirm units in the slicer and print a small pin and socket using your intended material and orientation. Adjust clearance for your setup.

02 / Cosplay fit

Plan armor that fits

Measure consistently

Take measurements over the intended underclothing; record units and check them again before scaling.

Allow for movement

Consider sitting, bending, ventilation, straps and overlaps. A static digital fit cannot guarantee comfort.

Make a small mock-up

Try a narrow sizing ring or low-material section first. Verify a known dimension in the slicer after export.
